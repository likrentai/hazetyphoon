The haze in numbers
Smoke = organic, brown and black carbon in NASA's GEOS-FP model, which reads about 1.8 times higher than Singapore's monitors (correlation 0.84). Wind: ERA5. Fires: NASA FIRMS VIIRS. Storms: IBTrACS. The project shows how far the smoke spread; it does not claim the smoke changed the storms.
